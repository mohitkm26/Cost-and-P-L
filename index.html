Period Lock

Admin only. Locking through a date blocks any new invoice with an entry date on or before it, and makes provisional costs booked on or before that date immutable on re-upload — finalizes the roll-forward for that period instead of leaving it perpetually open. Locking never overwrites a period that was already locked further forward.
